📐 Research Principles Applied to These Designs

  • Shield = Trust: Universal symbol of protection — used by Norton, McAfee, SSL providers. Non-negotiable for Trustit's brand.
  • Checkmark = Verified: Instant recognition of approval — used by Trustpilot, Google Verified, checkmark badges everywhere.
  • Interlocking = Community: Three pieces fitting together scores high on "unity" and "partnership" perception (circle psychology research).
  • Negative Space = Memorability: Hidden meanings (FedEx arrow, WWF panda) increase recall by 15% (MIT/Harvard study).
  • Single Line = Modern: 2024-2025 #1 trend — single continuous strokes are instantly recognizable and infinitely scalable.
  • Warm Tones = Approachability: Indian tier-2/3 consumers trust warm colors more than cold corporate blues (market research).
  • Seal/Stamp = Authority: "Official mark" psychology creates instant perception of verified authenticity.
  • Blue Dominance = Trust: #1 most trusted color globally — 87% of security brands use blue as primary (color psychology research).
