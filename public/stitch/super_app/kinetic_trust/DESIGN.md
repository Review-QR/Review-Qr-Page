## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-velocity, institution-grade consumer super-app tailored for the dynamic Indian digital landscape. It bridges hyper-local execution speed with uncompromising enterprise-level trust, giving consumers immediate reassurance across high-frequency daily services, verified micro-commerce, and high-stakes home tasks.

The visual ethos blends **Modern Hyper-Utility** with **Polished Tech-Pioneering**:
- **Uncompromised Fidelity & Legibility**: Crisp information hierarchy engineered for dense mobile viewports across varied ambient conditions—from direct Indian sunlight to night use.
- **Assurance Architecture**: Badges, verified checkmarks, and ratings hold strict spatial presence rather than decorative fluff, establishing immediate visual credibility.
- **Dynamic Speed**: Visuals leverage crisp, purposeful micro-transitions, streamlined edge geometries, and tactical color accents that denote active velocity without inducing clutter.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate psychological hierarchy anchored in institution-level security, punctuated by high-frequency digital vitality.

- **Primary (`#0D47A1` - Deep Royal Trust Blue)**: Dominates fundamental brand footprints, top app navigation headers, primary interaction triggers, and verified merchant authority anchors.
- **Secondary (`#00B4D8` - Electric Cyan)**: Used surgically for operational progression, live tracking bars, active delivery pings, instant service re-engagement, and flash action highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber Gold)**: Reserved strictly for verified ratings, prestige trust metrics, seller certification milestones, and reward milestones.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate)**: High-contrast typography master ensuring AA/AAA readability against `#FFFFFF` card layers and the overarching background canvas `#F8FAFC`.
- **System States**: Success utilizes vivid Emerald (`#10B981`) for completed UPI transactions and verified drops; Critical/Error employs Crimson (`#EF4444`) for booking delays and cancellation warnings.

## Typography

Typographic scale is optimized around **Plus Jakarta Sans** to combine contemporary geometric warmth with exceptional structural legibility at small sizes.

- **Indian Rupee (₹) Symbol Rule**: Every currency glyph must match the numeric font weight strictly, displayed with an explicit horizontal spacing buffer of `0.05em` from preceding characters to preserve immediate monetary cognition.
- **Numbers & Counters**: Tabular numbers (`tnum`) must be enforced for service dispatch counters, delivery timers, pricing listings, and verified review tally figures.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Dense data layers (such as service invoices and real-time tracking summaries) favor `body-sm` and `label-md` pairings to prevent cognitive fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8pt spatial grid system built for extreme mobile density, reflowing intelligently across larger form factors.

- **Mobile Canvas**: Follows a 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` (12px). Interactive targets must strictly respect a 48px baseline bounding box.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile Foldables**: Reflows to an 8-column layout with 24px margins, allowing side-by-side merchant category browsing and simultaneous order tracker trays.
- **Desktop Dashboard**: 12-column responsive layout capped at a maximum width of 1280px with centered margins, ensuring verified review matrices and multivariant carts do not overstretch.
- **Density Rules**: Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for metadata grouping inside service badges. Reserve `space-lg` (24px) exclusively for major vertical segment separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined multi-layer system that pairs soft, deeply diffused blue-tinted shadows with low-contrast slate bounding borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: Surface base `#F8FAFC`, non-elevated.
- **Level 1 (Standard Commerce Cards & Feeds)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `box-shadow: 0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.04), 0px 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` with an explicit 1px stroke of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active States, Floating Filters, Modals)**: Lifted white background supported by `box-shadow: 0px 12px 24px -6px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Trays & Hyper-Action Bars)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` supported by an upward diffusion: `box-shadow: 0px -8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.06)`.
- **Depth Exclusion**: Heavy dark dropshadows and unblended flat borders are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly yet precise physical contour.

- **Core Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px)**: Reserved for compact inputs, status indicators, counter controls, and nested sub-badges.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px)**: Applied to all product cards, booking tiles, merchant review groups, and contextual banners.
- **Hero Drawers & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 24px)**: Top-edge rounding on bottom modals, persistent floating order cards, and location pickers.
- **Capsule / Pill Geometry**: Strictly applied to verified trust badges, category chips, rating tags, and quick-filter pills to make them instantly scannable amid rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0D47A1` fill, crisp white typography (`label-lg`), minimum height of 48px, rounded to 12px. Interactive press state scales to `0.98` with background deepening to `#0A3880`.
- **Speed Action (Instant Buy / Reorder)**: Vibrant Electric Cyan (`#00B4D8`) fill with midnight neutral text (`#0F172A`) for maximum contextual pop.
- **Secondary / Tertiary**: Ghost border 1.5px using `#0D47A1` over transparent or faint tinted fills (`rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.04)`).

### Trust & Verification Badges
- **Verified Merchant**: Pill badge with deep royal blue tint background (`rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.08)`), containing a 14px royal blue seal checkmark icon and bold `label-sm` text reading "VERIFIED MERCHANT".
- **Verified Buyer Review**: Small pill containing amber tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), `#D97706` text, displaying solid star icon and verified buyer badge.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Default state: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, slate typography (`#334155`).
- Active state: `#0D47A1` solid fill, white text, zero border, with count badge enclosed in a micro-pill.

### Input Fields
- White fill, 1px stroke `#CBD5E1`, 48px height, 10px corner radius.
- Focus state: 1.5px border `#0D47A1` accompanied by a soft glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.12)`. Country code prefix `+91` fixed in place with distinct divider border.

### Super-App Cards
- Structured white surface, 16px border-radius, hairline stroke `#E2E8F0`. 
- Header contains clear service classification alongside ETA chips (e.g., "12 MINS" or "VERIFIED PRO"). Price tags feature bold Rupee symbols (`₹`) formatted with commas using the Indian numbering system (e.g., `₹1,24,500` or `₹499`).

### Live Order & Service Tracker Bar
- Compact persistent sheet docked to screen bottom with level-3 elevation, showing multi-stage radial progress using Electric Teal (`#00B4D8`) with status text and direct phone trigger.
