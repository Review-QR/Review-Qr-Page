Cancellation Policy Reference

If the kitchen has already acknowledged and printed the KOT ticket, the merchant is entitled to 100% full food preparation reimbursement. The customer is charged or platform escrow covers food wastage.
If an item is accepted but rejected later due to merchant stockout, a 10% restocking penalty applies unless the merchant contacted the customer within 120 seconds to arrange an approved substitute.
When food spillage occurs post-pickup, the 3PL fleet insurer is automatically debited. The merchant receives 100% net settlement and customer receives immediate refund with no negative merchant score impact.
If customer is unresponsive after 3 rider IVR calls and 10 minutes doorstep grace period, order is auto-closed as Customer Fault. Full payout is remitted to merchant.
