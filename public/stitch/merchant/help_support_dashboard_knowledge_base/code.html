account_tree How does EasySplit automatically pay 3PL couriers without touching escrow? expand_more

Under RBI Intermediary Directions and TRUSTIT Escrow architecture, when a delivery order confirms checkout, the total order amount splits instantly at payment authorization:

  • Merchant Food/Item Net: Direct route to Sri Krishna Gourmet Virtual Account (VA) ledger.
  • 3PL Delivery Fee + Surge: Programmatically locked and disbursed directly to the assigned logistics partner (Dunzo / Shadowfax) immediately after OTP verification at customer doorstep.
  • GST TDS / TCS Deductions: Segregated into the statutory government nodal withholding pool for automated monthly Form 27EQ and GSTR-8 matching.

receipt_long What happens if a customer claims non-delivery after KOT handover? expand_more

TRUSTIT enforces a Zero Merchant Liability protocol once hand-off occurs. Here is the dispute resolution workflow:

  1. When the 3PL rider scans the dispatch QR or enters the 4-digit POS token, merchant custody terminates.
  2. If the consumer flags non-receipt, the Rider GPS trajectory and customer doorstep geo-timestamp are audited automatically.
  3. The payout to your restaurant remains 100% protected and credited. Any customer refund is underwritten by the courier partner's transit insurance guarantee.
wifi_tethering_error SoundBox 4G is showing a yellow blinking LED: Step-by-step diagnostic expand_more

A yellow blinking LED indicates SIM network handshake latency or tower handoff failure:

Step 1

Hold Power button for 8s to force reboot.

Step 2

Wait for audio chime: "Connecting to Jio 4G".

Step 3

If still yellow, trigger Remote Ping from Portal.

If yellow persists over 15 minutes, click "Raise Hardware Replacement" to receive an immediate courier dispatch of a pre-configured SoundBox unit within 24 hours.
