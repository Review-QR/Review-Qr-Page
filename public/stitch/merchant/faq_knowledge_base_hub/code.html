SOP: Disputing an Unjustified Customer Complaint via CCTV & Weigh Scale Dossier

timer 4 mins read
task_alt Ombudsman Backed
1
Navigate to ‘Order Complaints’ and locate linked Order ID Portal Step

Open the left sidebar under Delivery Complaints & 3PL Fleet. Filter by the specific Order ID flagged within 2 hours of customer delivery.

2
Ensure KOT packing photograph shows the intact blue tamper seal Visual Proof

Your kitchen packing station image must clearly show the official serialized TRUSTIT Tamper-Evident Tape applied across the primary paper or plastic container seams.

3
Attach the calibrated weight slip from your Bluetooth IoT scale Telemetry Evidence

The automated scale record captures the exact net dispatch weight (±5 grams tolerance). If customer claims ‘items missing’, this IoT timestamp serves as conclusive legal proof.

4
Submit to Ombudsman Desk — Escrow safeguards payout within 60 minutes Final Resolution

Click ‘Dispatch Dispute Dossier’. The platform freeze on the settlement amount is instantly unlocked, and 100% funds route to your merchant bank account without clawbacks.
