# 🛡️ Trustit Merchant Companion (Chrome Extension)

Chrome Extension specially built for **Trustit Merchants** to quickly access their dashboard, 8 QR standee posters, review link, and business stats directly from Google Chrome!

---

## 🚀 How to Install in 30 Seconds (Chrome Desktop):

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. In the address bar, type:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
   and press **Enter**.
3. At the top-right corner, turn ON **Developer mode** toggle.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** (लोड अनपैक्ड) button in the top-left.
5. Select the `extension` folder from this project (`E:\Project-Trustit\extension`).
6. Click **Select Folder**.
7. 🎉 **Done!** The Trustit shield icon will appear in your Chrome extensions bar!
8. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) next to your URL bar and click the **Pin** icon next to **Trustit Merchant Companion** so it stays visible all the time.

---

## ⚡ What You Can Do with 1 Click:
- **Open Dashboard**: Directly opens `trustitreview.com/merchant/dashboard`.
- **My 8 QR Standees**: Direct shortcut to your 8 category standee templates.
- **Copy Review Link**: Instantly copies your business review link to share on WhatsApp or SMS with customers.
